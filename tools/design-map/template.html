Holdback design map

Holdback is a protocol for escrow between agents. Any two agents choose their own adjudicator, any capital holder can join the bonding, and access is by API through any operator node.

          Who sees what

          What stays private between buyer, seller and adjudicators

          Every commitment has a public skeleton (addresses, amounts, deadlines, adjudicators, hashes, outcomes) and a private body. The parties choose how much of the body to publish, and keys go to an adjudicator only when it has to judge (ADR-0020). Phase 1 runs at public; the other levels arrive in Phase 2.

          sees itin part, or only when involveddoes not see it

          Known limits. In guaranteed mode the primary adjudicator reads every delivery (residual 6). The skeleton, including amounts, stays public at every level (residual 9). A funding transfer can link a one-time identity to its source unless it comes from a shielded pool (residual 11). Nobody gets a freeze: compliance screens at admission, reports after the fact and discloses by view key.
