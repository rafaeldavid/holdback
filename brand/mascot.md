# The mule

> **Status (2026-11-03): superseded.** The brand is ’crow, with Thorburn's 1919 hooded crow as its mark (`brand/README.md`). This file is kept as history.

Rafa's choice (2026-10-31): the mascot is a mule, as in *carries the load*.

## Why a mule fits

| Mule trait | What it says about Holdback |
| --- | --- |
| Carries the load for others | The hold carries the buyer's money and the seller's sealed work across the deal, so the two agents don't have to trust each other |
| Famously stubborn | Neutral. It goes where the signed terms point and nobody can push it elsewhere: not an operator, an adjudicator, a club or Holdback's own maintainers |
| Sure-footed on rough ground | Settles reliably when things go wrong: an operator refuses, an adjudicator goes quiet, a deliverable carries an injection (demo beats 6 and 7) |
| Born of two different parents | Sits between two parties from different firms and belongs to neither |
| A working animal, not a show animal | Infrastructure. Plain, dependable, priced on work done |
| Hardy and cheap to keep | Machine-speed and low-cost per commitment |

## Character

- **Personality:** calm, patient, unbothered, quietly competent. Never flashy, never in a hurry, never takes sides.
- **What it does in pictures:** carries a strongbox or a sealed crate on a pack saddle; stands still while two agents argue; walks a narrow path between two cliffs; plants its hooves when pushed.
- **What it never does:** run, wink, take a bribe, carry anything for only one side, or look like a meme coin mascot.

## The one risk to manage: "money mule"

"Money mule" is a standard anti-money-laundering term for someone who moves illicit funds for others. Banks, regulators and fraud teams use it daily, and an escrow product that moves money is exactly where they will make the link.

How to manage it:

1. **Always "pack mule", never "money mule".** The image is a working animal carrying a sealed load. Never draw cash, coins or bags with currency signs on the mule.
2. **Lead with the work, not the money.** The mule carries the *hold*: a sealed crate standing for the commitment, the work and the payment together.
3. **Never write "mule" next to "money", "funds", "transfer" or "cash"** in copy. "Carries the load" yes; "moves your money" no.
4. **Check with a compliance reader** before the mascot goes in front of banks, ramps or regulators.

If the risk proves too costly, other load-bearers keep the idea: an **ox** (patient strength, yoked between two), a **tortoise** (carries its shelter, slow and certain), a **pack camel** (crosses hostile ground), or an **ant** (carries many times its weight, works in a colony). The mule stays the first choice until that check.

## Usage

- The mark (`mule.svg`) is a side view of a mule carrying a sealed crate. It works at 24 px as an icon and large as an illustration.
- Colour: one ink colour on a light or dark ground; the crate may take the accent colour.
- Keep it out of error states that blame a party. The mule is neutral, so it appears for "held", "settled" and "waiting", not for "rejected".
