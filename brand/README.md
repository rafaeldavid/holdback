## Rules for anything written here

- Claim only what the build does. Label anything not yet built as direction, not fact: the demo runs on SimChain with scripted judges and no real money.
- Use the product vocabulary from `docs/architecture.md` (Glossary). Keep the chain behind the API: lead with holds, judgment and receipts, not tokens or blockchains.
- Names, people and firms in examples are invented and labelled as such.
- "Holdback" is still a working name.
