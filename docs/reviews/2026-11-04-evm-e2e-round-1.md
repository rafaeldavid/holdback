## Findings by severity

Each failure gets its severity, what is wrong, where, the impact, the evidence, and a backlog row or task. Live findings come with the live runs.

### Medium · A relayed step can land on chain without the operators logging it

- **What:** in the homepage run relayed by api.escrow.stream on 2026-11-04, deal `0xf0453986b888caa632f81606bcc44b28c24f0503b25c990851ae736091405e5b` (with Crow's test seller):
  - the operators' relayer lane `0x5e7f1a8c…3243` sent the seller's `deliver` in [`0x64c5c757…caf1`](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x64c5c757d4af4fd9b40850435eefffacdabe184835bad79b36282f7addd0caf1) (block 12,064,917);
  - neither operator's log has the deal's `delivery.submitted` or the key release to the primary;
  - the primary still decided (block 12,064,918) and the deal released.
- **Where:** `packages/settlement/evm/evm-settlement.ts`, `send`, after the receipt [inferred]. It sets the book's state, then reads `dealOf` at the latest block through one of several RPCs (`apps/demo-cloud/src/worker.ts`). A node a block behind answers the old stage, the divergence check throws, and the step's events are never emitted, while the book already holds the step.
- **Impact:** the money and the buyer's key are right on chain. But the operators do not serve the ciphertext pointer, so the buyer cannot open the work through the API, and the homepage command fails. It happened in 1 of the run's 4 deals [measured].
- **Evidence:** both operators' event logs, and the deal's transactions and `JobSubmitted` log read from Sepolia with the driver's `RoundChain` [measured].
- **Fix:** read `dealOf` at the receipt's block, and set the book's state only after the checks pass, or emit the events whatever a later read says. T-218's log sync may not repair it, because the book already shows the stage. Backlog row, Settlement layer; a fix task before T-220's deploy.

### Low · `appeal-expiry` through the API can answer 500 on anvil (not on Sepolia)

- **On Sepolia** both deals' `appeal-expiry` went through the API (80,792 gas each) [measured, first live run]. What follows is anvil only.

- **What:** on anvil 1.0.0, `POST /v1/commitments/<id>/triggers/appeal-expiry` answered 500 `internal` for both deals of one offline run. The operator's relay simulates each call with `eth_estimateGas`, which anvil refused for `expireAppeal` with "gas floor exceeds the gas limit", while `eth_call` passed at 200,000 gas.
- **Where:** `packages/settlement/evm/live.ts` (the estimate) and `evm-settlement.ts` (a non-revert error is thrown, not refused).
- **Impact:** when it happens, nobody's relayed expiry lands, the demo adjudicators' own expiries included (T-211), so their bonds stay encumbered until someone calls `expireAppeal` directly with a fixed gas limit. In a fresh world the same expiries succeeded at 54,773 gas, so it depends on the call's bytes. The likely cause is execution gas below the EIP-7623 calldata floor (inferred).
- **Evidence:** the driver's anvil runs, 2026-11-04 [measured]; the direct call with a fixed limit used 59,561 gas and freed the primary's 25 tUSDC bond.
- **Next:** scenario 1's live run shows whether Sepolia's RPC does the same. The backlog row (Demo section) holds the fix.

### Info · CPP keeps a refundable amount after settlement that nothing can use

- **What:** after each settled deal on anvil, CPP's `paymentState` reads `capturableAmount` 0 and `refundableAmount` equal to what it captured (26, 1 and 15 tUSDC in three deals) [measured]. Only CPP's operator, the rules contract, may refund, and `HoldbackRules` never calls `refund` (it calls `authorize`, `capture` and `void` only) [inferred from `contracts/src/HoldbackRules.sol`].
- **Impact:** none on the funds. A reader of CPP alone may think the captured price can still return to the buyer. The round's check "CPP holds nothing" reads the capturable amount.
