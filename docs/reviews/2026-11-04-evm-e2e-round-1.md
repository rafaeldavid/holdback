## Summary

Round 1 is complete. Every settlement path the round set out to test ended where the signed terms said, on the live demo [measured: the 8 run records]:
- release, refund on reject, reclaim through the API and from the buyer's key, the default outcome, appeal expiry and offer expiry;
- the fallback with a drand quicknet proof, escalation to a default and escalation decided on appeal;
- appeals upheld, and appeals overturned both ways with the price paid from the first decider's bond (D43);
- the escape hatch (funding, delivery and the fallback takeover sent from the parties' own keys), early accept, and mutual settlement before and after delivery.

The results:
- **15 of 16 scenarios passed live with every check.** Scenario 11, a restart between broadcast and receipt, passed 12 of 13 checks and counts by its coverage (D54, item 1).
- **Finality is clean.** On 2026-11-06 from 21:03 to 21:05 UTC, the driver re-read all 153 transactions of the 8 runs, and every one sat in its recorded block.
- **The round found two product defects, both fixed and live.** A relayed step could land on chain without the operators logging it (Medium, T-226, live with T-220's deploy). The operators dropped a seller's posted copy of a direct delivery (High, T-230, live since 2026-11-06 12:19 UTC).
- **Every other failure was the round's own.** The driver read lagging RPC nodes (fixed in PRs 210 and 223), two runs overlapped on the round's keys (fixed in PR 223), and scenario 11's live redeploy is too slow for its 12 s window (redesign in T-310, Phase 3).
- **The passes span three operator builds.** Rafa accepted them as they are on 2026-11-06 ("Accept passes as is", D55), so no rerun is needed for Phase 2.
- **Low, new:** the fixed gas quote in the operator fee covers every path without a drand-proven decision and none of the paths with one (Gas per path).

- An upheld appeal costs the appellant its 1.25 tUSDC appeal bond. An overturn pays the appellant the price from the primary's bond, and the appeal bond comes back.
