## Findings by severity

Each failure gets its severity, what is wrong, where, the impact, the evidence, and a backlog row or task. Live findings come with the live runs.

### Low · `appeal-expiry` through the API can answer 500 on anvil

- **What:** on anvil 1.0.0, `POST /v1/commitments/<id>/triggers/appeal-expiry` answered 500 `internal` for both deals of one offline run. The operator's relay simulates each call with `eth_estimateGas`, which anvil refused for `expireAppeal` with "gas floor exceeds the gas limit", while `eth_call` passed at 200,000 gas.
- **Where:** `packages/settlement/evm/live.ts` (the estimate) and `evm-settlement.ts` (a non-revert error is thrown, not refused).
- **Impact:** when it happens, nobody's relayed expiry lands, the demo adjudicators' own expiries included (T-211), so their bonds stay encumbered until someone calls `expireAppeal` directly with a fixed gas limit. In a fresh world the same expiries succeeded at 54,773 gas, so it depends on the call's bytes. The likely cause is execution gas below the EIP-7623 calldata floor (inferred).
- **Evidence:** the driver's anvil runs, 2026-11-04 [measured]; the direct call with a fixed limit used 59,561 gas and freed the primary's 25 tUSDC bond.
- **Next:** scenario 1's live run shows whether Sepolia's RPC does the same. The backlog row (Demo section) holds the fix.

### Info · CPP keeps a refundable amount after settlement that nothing can use

- **What:** after each settled deal on anvil, CPP's `paymentState` reads `capturableAmount` 0 and `refundableAmount` equal to what it captured (26, 1 and 15 tUSDC in three deals) [measured]. Only CPP's operator, the rules contract, may refund, and `HoldbackRules` never calls `refund` (it calls `authorize`, `capture` and `void` only) [inferred from `contracts/src/HoldbackRules.sol`].
- **Impact:** none on the funds. A reader of CPP alone may think the captured price can still return to the buyer. The round's check "CPP holds nothing" reads the capturable amount.
