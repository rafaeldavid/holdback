## Intent

Make the public copy say what the Crow ontology settled (proposed in the private note `strategy/2026-11-14-crow-ontology.md`, reviewed by Rafa as a before/after table on 2026-11-14). The person or company behind an agent is its principal, and "owner" stays for addresses and keys. The money waits in a payment hold with its release rule, not a vault. The deciding service is "the reviewer both sides chose" on people pages, since nothing yet makes it independent: Crow runs the reference adjudicator. The record is the deal's. Nothing is renamed in the spec, the contracts or any URL.
