# ADR-0028: The public demo settles on Sepolia

- **Status:** Proposed, 2026-11-03. Rafa decided the move (D41: "the public demo to settle on Sepolia"); the design below is decided under "autonomy inside the repo" and Rafa may overrule any line.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03
- **Research:** `docs/research/2026-11-03-sepolia-demo-settlement.md` (Fable 5.1, maximum effort; measurements of Sepolia, its public RPCs, drand and Cloudflare's limits).
- **Builds on:** ADR-0002 (custody on the neutral settlement layer), ADR-0003 (CPP base), ADR-0005 (operators relay signed messages), ADR-0018 (beacon), ADR-0023 (operator fee), ADR-0025 (EVM funding and token identifiers, Proposed), ADR-0026 (funding paths); T-202, T-205, T-206, T-209.
- **Changes:** `packages/settlement/evm` (a live-chain sender, SimChain-shaped events, adjudicator envelopes, books a store can keep), `apps/demo-cloud` (the flag, storage, setup, faucet), `apps/operator` (the domain's verifying contract, ERC-3009 messages in prepare), `examples/try.ts` and `verify.ts`, `site/llms.txt`, `docs/operations.md`. No change to `spec/schemas/` or the contracts.

## Context

The public demo (api.escrow.stream and api2.escrow.stream) settles on SimChain: a simulation of the escrow rules inside one Cloudflare Durable Object. The contracts that would hold real custody (HoldbackRules on Coinbase's AuthCaptureEscrow, HoldbackBonds, the drand verifier) exist and pass the same 29 scenario fixtures on a local chain (T-205), but nobody outside the repository can see them work.

On Sepolia, the money in every demo deal sits in those contracts. Anyone can check the hold, the release and the key release on Etherscan, and anyone with Sepolia ETH can call the contracts without an operator. That makes the neutrality claim checkable, not just stated.

What changes for an agent: each money step waits for a block (about 12 s), so a deal takes about a minute instead of 4 s; the EIP-712 domain names chain 11155111 and the rules contract; the token identifier is the test token's address (ADR-0025); and the buyer's funding is the token's own ERC-3009 signature. What must not change: the API, the one-command test deal (`try.mjs`) and its single approval, and the hard rules (no real money, no admin keys, operators relay and never author, secrets from the environment only).

## Decision

1. **One flag per Worker, one world per setting.** Each Worker has `SETTLEMENT`, `sim` or `sepolia`. The Worker forwards to the world named for it (`holdback-public-demo-2026-11-02` for SimChain, unchanged; `holdback-public-demo-sepolia-2026-11-03` for Sepolia). The Durable Object takes its kind from the name the Worker set, records it in the world's `meta` when it is created, and refuses to open as the other kind. The SimChain world's storage is never touched, so setting the flag back is the rollback.
2. **The same API; the chain holds the money.** The world runs `EvmSettlement` (kind `testnet`) over a live chain instead of SimChain.
   - Before funding, the offer, the adjudicator's acceptance and the agreement are signatures the adapter collects and checks as SimChain does; `agreeAndFund` verifies all three on chain (T-202 rule 1).
   - From funding on, every state change is one contract call and the contract is the authority: a revert is the refusal, a mined call the change. The adapter steps a core mirror at the block's time and checks it against `dealOf` after every call.
   - Events keep SimChain's payloads, so the operator, the adjudicators and agents read either backend the same way, plus `tx`, `block`, `sender` and `gasUsed` for the transaction that made them. The primary's acceptance is in its event in full, so anyone holding the signed terms can fund without an operator.
   - The adapter holds the delivery's envelopes for the adjudicators and releases each at its turn (primary at delivery, fallback at takeover, appeal at escalation or appeal), as SimChain does (ADR-0020); the buyer's is released on chain in the paying transaction. Measured on anvil: all 25 contract fixtures that run (3 excluded until Phase 4) now have transcripts identical to SimChain's (6 of 29 before). One payload name still differs: the adapter's `adjudicator.accepted` says `bondRequired` (with `freeBond`), SimChain's says `bondReserved`; transcripts do not show it.
3. **Relayer lanes pay gas; they relay and never author.** One Worker secret derives a funding lane and three deal lanes (Sepolia-only keys). A deal keeps one lane by its commitment ID; each lane sends one transaction at a time and waits for its receipt, so a lane's nonce is always "last mined plus one" and one stuck transaction delays one lane. The funding lane mints for the faucet, sets up the adjudicators and tops up the deal lanes. Gas: `eth_estimateGas` plus 25 %, capped at 3 million (1.5 million until Sepolia was measured: there new state costs about 3.5 times Prague's, and the funding bundle takes 938,547 gas, measured 2026-11-03 with `eth_simulateV1`); faucet mints go six to a Multicall3 call. Fees: twice the base fee plus the median tip, refused above a cap (20 gwei) with `relay_refused`, which tells the agent the signed message is still valid elsewhere. The contracts never read `msg.sender`, give the relayer no role and check every signature, so the relayer can choose when and whether to relay, nothing else. The gas is part of the operator fee (D33 as amended on 2026-11-03, ADR-0023's gas amendment): on Sepolia the published minimum is 4.71 tUSDC, a 4.70 tUSDC gas quote plus the 0.01 relay minimum, at a fixed and fictional demo rate (first set at 1.50 from anvil's gas, before Sepolia was measured). When anyone relays the buyer's fee transfer ahead of the bundle, the adapter funds with `agreeAndFund` alone (ADR-0023's EVM mitigation, implemented here), but only when the token's `AuthorizationUsed` log shows that transfer paid this operator. A fee nonce spent any other way (paid to someone else or, on USDC, cancelled with `cancelAuthorization`) is `payment_required`: the spent state alone proves nothing (public-audit cross-check, 2026-11-03).
4. **A write answers at inclusion.** The response comes after the transaction's receipt (one confirmation), as today's clients expect (`try.ts` checks `Held` in the agree response). The events name the transaction; finality (about 15 minutes) is not awaited and the docs say so. A reorg deep enough to drop a mined call is a residual risk on Sepolia, stated in the docs.
5. **Storage a restart can open without a replay.** A Sepolia world stores each commitment's book in one row, the event log in pages of 64 events and admitted mandates in pages; opening reads those rows and replays nothing (the November 2 outage was a journal replay against the free read quota). The alarm runs every 2 s while adjudicator work is pending and every 12 s otherwise while the world is active.
6. **The token is Crow Test USDC (tUSDC), and the faucet mints it on chain.** D42 makes the first wave USDC only; the demo uses our own open-mint test token with USDC's ERC-3009 interface (`contracts/script/TestUSDC.sol`: no owner, pause, blacklist or upgrade), named as a test token, not Circle's Sepolia USDC, whose issuer can freeze an address and whose faucet a person must drive (measured, D42). The funding lane mints the grant (it guards no value) and answers after the receipt, batching grants that arrive together into one Multicall3 call. The per-address and per-client limits stay; a daily mint budget is added. Two requests for one address that arrive together get one grant: the world's one Durable Object holds the grants in progress and the day's count in memory (red-team finding, 2026-11-03).
7. **The demo adjudicators keep real bonds.** A setup pass, idempotent against on-chain reads, gives each of the three adjudicators Sepolia ETH for its own `approve` and `deposit` (HoldbackBonds bonds `msg.sender`) and 25,000 tUSDC of bond, as on SimChain. Until a bond is in place that adjudicator's offers wait.
8. **Funding on EVM follows ADR-0025 under today's schema.** `prepare/agree-and-fund` returns, for `fund.signature`, an ERC-3009 `ReceiveWithAuthorization` under the token's own EIP-712 domain (to the escrow's collector, value price plus fee, valid from 0 until `fundBy`, nonce the payer-agnostic PaymentInfo hash of the terms), and for `operatorFee.signature` a `TransferWithAuthorization` whose nonce is bound to the commitment. The body's `scheme` stays `"erc3009"` (T-205 decision 3) until schema v0.9 names it `cpp-erc3009`. Both nonces are fixed by the terms: an agent may leave them out or send that value. `examples/verify.ts` recomputes them from the pinned deployment before it signs.
9. **Pinned domains.** `llms.txt` and `try.mjs` pin the Sepolia domain (`chainId` 11155111, `verifyingContract` the rules contract) and the deployment's addresses. `try.mjs` also accepts SimChain's domain, so a rollback does not break it.
10. **RPC.** viem's fallback over two keyless endpoints that accepted batches in testing (publicnode, Tenderly's gateway), with an optional keyed URL from a secret placed first. Polled reads are served from the mirror and a short balance cache, never from the chain.
11. **Staged after this change** (each a task of its own, stated as a limit until it lands):
    - **drand proofs for fallback and appeal decisions** (ADR-0018 §4, T-209): a `drand-quicknet` randomness source in the adjudicator service with local BLS verification, the core round arithmetic per source, and the parse rule for chain settlement. Until then a fallback or appeal adjudicator on Sepolia cannot prove its seed, so it does not decide and the default outcome applies (ADR-0018 §5).
    - **Direct transactions in the operators' view.** The rules contract's logs, synced each tick, for transactions no operator relayed; today a direct call settles on chain while the operators' view lags it. That includes a watcher copying the bundle's fund leg alone out of the mempool: the deal funds without the fee and the node's bundle reverts. Once the view syncs, the node can still send the fee leg alone until `fundBy` (public-audit cross-check, `contracts/test/crosscheck/BundleAtomicity.t.sol` on its branch).
    - **An in-flight row per lane**, so a Durable Object restarted between broadcast and receipt picks up its own transaction.
    - **Schema v0.9 items** (T-033): the `cpp-erc3009` scheme name, a `chain` object on the state read, settlement contracts in discovery, `relayer_unavailable`.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not (now) |
| --- | --- |
| Keep SimChain | Rafa's decision; the contracts are ready and the claim is checkable only on a public chain |
| One Durable Object per operator node, each reading the chain | Real independence, but the pre-funding stages are off chain and would need to be gossiped between nodes; a separate deployment, later |
| One relayer key with several transactions in flight | More throughput per key, but a dropped transaction stalls every later nonce, and an evicted object cannot know what is in flight (research §2) |
| Agents pay their own gas | Honest and simplest, but breaks the one-command trial; it stays the escape hatch |
| Carry the delivery in the decision (two transactions, D4) | Saves 61k gas, but leaves the deal `Held` on chain until the decision; on the demo every state should map to a chain event |
| Wait for `safe` or finality before answering | 6 to 17 minutes per step |
| A hosted relayer (OpenZeppelin, Gelato) | A third party and an account in the write path |

## Consequences

- **Latency:** about 60 to 100 s per test deal (research §10, inferred); each money step about 12 to 24 s.
- **Cost:** about 0.0016 Sepolia ETH per test deal: about 1.56 million gas for its three transactions at Sepolia's 1 gwei median tip, with a base fee near zero (both measured 2026-11-03), plus about 0.0005 for the two faucet mints. Rafa funded the funding lane (1.94 Sepolia ETH on 2026-11-03, measured by the coordinating session); faucets and CAPTCHAs are his, never an agent's. The buyer pays it back in the operator fee, in tUSDC at the demo's fixed rate.
- **Quota:** the storage layout and the slower alarm keep a busy day inside the free plan's limits by a wide margin (inferred); the account is reportedly on Workers Paid since the November 2 outage (not checked from here), so a busy day fails soft.
- **Appeals after T-206's H-2 fix:** the contract now refuses a decision that carries the buyer's key unless it is an accept on a deal that is not Refunded, and the reference adjudicator attaches the key to every accept, so its overturn-to-accept party appeals revert on Sepolia until T-211 lands. Appeals on the demo also need the appellant's own Sepolia ETH (the bond allowance); both are stated limits.
- **Honest limits** change in `llms.txt`: real settlement, worthless open-mint token; two names, one process; what the operators still hold (relayer and adjudicator keys, bonds, pre-funding stages, envelopes in transit, judgment records); what Etherscan shows and what it does not; unaudited contracts; your own gas for appeals and direct calls; inclusion, not finality.
- **Rollback:** `SETTLEMENT=sim` on both Workers and a deploy (`docs/operations.md`); the SimChain world resumes where it stopped.

## Litmus tests

1. **Neutrality (ADR-0001).** Pass, with the residuals above. No operator can author or alter a message: the contract checks every signature and gives the relayer no role. Any party with Sepolia ETH can submit the same signed messages to HoldbackRules itself (the escape hatch, measured on anvil in T-205), and the primary's acceptance is now served so funding can go direct too. The operators still hold the pre-funding stages and the adjudicators' envelopes off chain; an operator that withholds them delays a deal that has not been funded, and the parties hold their own signed copies. No admin key anywhere: the contracts have no owner, and the relayer key is a Sepolia-only gas payer.
2. **The hold (ADR-0002).** Pass. From funding on, the money is in CPP's escrow under HoldbackRules on Sepolia: neither party can take it alone, every hold expires (`authorizationExpiry`), operations are idempotent, and a double funding is refused by CPP. The faucet and setup only mint the open-mint test token.
3. **Defended judgment (ADR-0006).** Unchanged for the primary. Fallback and appeal decisions need drand proofs, staged above; until they land, a silent primary ends in the default outcome rather than an unproven seed (ADR-0018 §5).
